Visual Standards

OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL FOR MULTIMEDIA CREW
01

Typography & Scale

Legibility from the back row of the sanctuary is paramount. Never crowd slides with excess characters.

  • Headings / Song Titles: 48pt – 54pt (Bold weights required).
  • Body / Lyrics / Verses: 60pt – 72pt. Limit enough lines to avoid exceeding the screen width.
  • Fonts: Bebas Neue for overall.
  • Cue Styling: Red for instrumentals, Light Green for Highlights and Repetitions.
02

Approved Color System

Maintain high contrast colors for consistent usage of prompter palettes.

Background [constant] #0C0C10
Lyrics #FFF
Song Structures #FFCD00
Instrumental Cues #FB2943
Repetition Cues #CCFFCC
03

Live Service Execution

  • Contrast Rule: Ensure crisp white or gold text over dark backgrounds. Avoid mid-tone gray-on-gray palettes.
  • Visual Margins: Keep text well clear of display borders. Maintain a safe padding zone around all slide elements.
  • Background Control: Use clean, distraction-free gradients or approved motion loops that support rather than obscure the text content.
